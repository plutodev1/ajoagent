# Product: AjoAgent

AjoAgent runs Ajo/Esusu rotating savings circles onchain on Celo.
- Members contribute a fixed amount of USD₮ each round to the agent wallet.
- When everyone has paid, the agent converts the pot to cNGN on Textile FX and pays the member whose turn it is.
- Users are Nigerian savings groups (organiser + members), many new to crypto. Plain language, naira-first, mobile-first.
- Trust is the product: every contribution and payout must be visible and verifiable (Celoscan links, tagged txs).
